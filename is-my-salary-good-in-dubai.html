Is my salary good in Dubai?

Updated 4 August 2026 · The UAE Wealth Dashboard · Methodology and sources

A Dubai salary is “good” at roughly AED 20,000 a month for a single person and AED 35,000–40,000 for a family with school-age children. But the honest answer depends on two things: where you rank among comparable professionals, and what rent takes out of your pay.

Rent is the deciding variable. A typical Dubai one-bedroom costs AED 68,000–82,000 a year and private school fees commonly run AED 40,000–90,000 per child. The standard local guidance is to keep rent under about 30% of gross pay.

Should I benchmark against my role or against the average?

Comparing yourself to “the average Dubai salary” is misleading — the average mixes a construction labourer on AED 2,000 with a banker on AED 150,000. The useful comparison is the published range for your exact job title, adjusted for your experience and emirate. The dashboard's salary check does this against 837 benchmarked roles and returns a verdict: below market, at market, or above market.

How do I know if I am underpaid in Dubai?

Practical indicators: your package sits below the bottom of your role's published range; you have more than five years of experience but sit in the lower half of the range; your pay has not moved in two years while market increases ran 2.6% in 2025; or peers in the same role at comparable firms report materially higher packages. UAE salary growth is forecast at about 1.6% for 2026, so switching employers remains the main route to a significant step up.

How much does the lack of income tax change the comparison?

A lot. AED 20,000 a month is AED 240,000 a year and, with no income tax and no employee social contributions for expatriate staff, all of it is take-home. Matching that take-home elsewhere needs a much larger gross salary: roughly £66,000 in the UK and $81,000 in the United States on federal tax alone, more like $90,000 in a state that levies its own income tax. Those are equivalence estimates, not offers: they assume a single filer on standard allowances, a dirham pegged at 3.6725 to the dollar and sterling around $1.34, and they say nothing about cost of living, which in Dubai is high on housing and schooling and low on almost everything else.

Frequently asked questions

Is AED 15,000 a good salary in Dubai?
AED 15,000 a month places you in roughly the top 15% of all UAE workers. It comfortably supports a single person in a one-bedroom apartment with a car, but is tight for a family with children in private school.
Is AED 25,000 a good salary in Dubai?
Yes. It puts you in about the top 8% of all UAE workers and above the median professional. It supports a two-bedroom apartment and a comfortable family lifestyle, though private school fees for two children would still stretch it.
How much should I ask for in a Dubai salary negotiation?
Anchor to the published range for your job title and your years of experience rather than to your current pay. If you sit below the midpoint of your role's range and have five or more years of experience, the midpoint is a defensible ask. Confirm whether the offer is all-inclusive or basic plus allowances, since that changes gratuity calculations.
