UAE net worth percentiles

Updated 4 August 2026 · The UAE Wealth Dashboard · Methodology and sources

The top 1% of UAE adults hold net worth above about AED 14,419,108 ($3,926,238), while the modelled median adult holds around AED 337,549. The UAE's wealth Gini of 0.82 is the highest in the world, tied with Russia, so wealth percentiles rise very steeply near the top.

Why are there two different UAE median net worth figures?

UBS's survey-based median wealth per UAE adult was $32,746 in 2023, well below the $91,913 used in the model on this site, which follows World Inequality Database methodology. Survey-based measures under-count both the low-wage migrant population and the very top of the distribution. Mean wealth per adult was $157,612 in 2025 — roughly five times the survey median, which is itself a measure of how concentrated UAE wealth is.

Frequently asked questions

How much money do you need to be in the top 1% in the UAE?
About AED 14,419,108 ($3,926,238) in net worth. Knight Frank's separate estimate puts the UAE top-1% wealth entry point at roughly $1.6 million, measured on a different basis.
What is the average net worth in the UAE?
Mean wealth per adult was $157,612 in 2025 according to UBS. The median is far lower — $32,746 on UBS's survey basis — because wealth is highly concentrated.
How many millionaires are there in the UAE?
About 183,000 US-dollar millionaires as of the UBS Global Wealth Report 2026, which revised the series; the prior edition reported 240,343 on 2024 data. Roughly 81,200 of them live in Dubai.
