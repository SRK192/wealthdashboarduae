What is a good salary in Dubai for a family?

Updated 5 August 2026 · The UAE Wealth Dashboard · Methodology and sources

A family of four in Dubai needs roughly AED 15,278 a month for a workable life on the cheaper school curricula, and around AED 24,722 if the children attend British-curriculum schools. That upper figure is above the 59.7th percentile of UAE household income, so most households do not reach it.

What does a family of four actually need in Dubai?

Two costs decide it and everything else is noise around them: housing and schooling. A two-bedroom apartment in an affordable community such as Al Nahda or International City runs AED 72,000 to AED 82,000 a year. The same apartment in Business Bay or Dubai Marina runs AED 148,000 to AED 157,000, and in Downtown Dubai about AED 223,000. There is no public-school option for expatriate children, so school fees are a fixed cost rather than a choice.

Keeping rent and fees together inside 60% of gross household income, which is already aggressive by the standards of most budgeting guidance, gives roughly AED 15,278 a month with two children on the Indian curriculum and about AED 24,722 a month with two on the British curriculum.

What share of Dubai households actually earn that?

This is the question the budget guides never answer. Median UAE household income is AED 19,720 a month. The lower of the two figures above sits at the 38.4th percentile of household income and the higher at the 59.7th percentile. In other words, the comfortable expatriate family life that dominates the way Dubai is described from outside is available to a minority of households, and the arithmetic above is why.

How much are school fees in Dubai?

Annual fees per child average about AED 14,000 on the Indian curriculum, AED 42,000 on the American, AED 48,000 on the British and AED 65,000 on the IB, with individual schools spanning a very wide range within each. For two children the difference between the cheapest and the most expensive curriculum is on the order of AED 102,000 a year, which is more than many households earn in total. Curriculum choice is the single largest financial decision an expatriate family in Dubai makes.

Is one income enough, or do both parents need to work?

On a single income, the AED 24,722 a month needed for two children in British-curriculum schools is top 14% of workers in conventional households and top 38% of white-collar professionals. It is reachable on one salary in senior professional roles and not otherwise. The cheaper configuration, at AED 15,278, is top 28% of household-frame workers, which is why dual income is the norm rather than the exception among families that stay long term.

Does moving to Sharjah or Ajman change the answer?

Substantially, on housing. A one-bedroom in Sharjah runs AED 37,000 to AED 43,000 a year against AED 56,000 to AED 61,000 in affordable Dubai, and Ajman is cheaper again at AED 27,000 to AED 30,000. On this site's own emirate index, professional pay in Sharjah runs about 5% below Dubai and Ajman about 10% below, so the trade only really works if the job stays in Dubai and you take the commute. Many families do exactly that. See salaries and wealth by emirate for the full comparison.

Where do these figures come from?

Rents are from the Bayut Dubai Rental Market Report for the first half of 2026. School fees are Dubai 2026-27 curriculum averages. Household income percentiles come from this site's own model, published openly as a free CSV and JSON dataset. The about page sets out the method and the limitations.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good salary in Dubai for a family of 4?
Roughly AED 15,278 a month covers a two-bedroom in an affordable community plus two children on the Indian curriculum. About AED 24,722 a month is needed for the same housing with two children in British-curriculum schools. The higher figure sits at the 59.7th percentile of UAE household income.
How much are school fees in Dubai per child?
About AED 14,000 a year on average for the Indian curriculum, AED 42,000 for the American, AED 48,000 for the British and AED 65,000 for the IB, with a wide range inside each. There is no public-school option for expatriate children.
How much rent does a family of four pay in Dubai?
A two-bedroom apartment costs AED 72,000 to AED 82,000 a year in affordable communities such as Al Nahda and International City, AED 148,000 to AED 157,000 in Business Bay or Dubai Marina, and about AED 223,000 in Downtown Dubai.
Can a family live in Dubai on one salary?
On the cheaper school curricula, yes, but the required income is top 28% of workers in conventional households, so it takes a solid professional salary. With British-curriculum schooling the requirement rises to about AED 24,722 a month, which single-income households reach only in senior roles.
