Operations Manager salary in the UAE

Updated 3 August 2026 · The UAE Wealth Dashboard · Methodology and sources

A operations manager in the UAE earns between AED 12,000 and AED 41,400 a month, with a midpoint of AED 26,700. That midpoint sits at the 92.6th percentile of all UAE workers, and the top 34% of white-collar professionals.

What a operations manager earns in the UAE

Across the 3 operations manager entries in our dataset, drawn from published recruiter salary guides and reduced by 10% because those guides describe packages rather than base pay, the range runs AED 12,000 to AED 41,400 a month. The midpoint is AED 26,700 a month, or AED 320,400 a year.

Where that ranks in the UAE

A operations manager on the midpoint of AED 26,700 sits at the 92.6th percentile of all UAE workers, putting the role in the top 7.4% of everyone employed in the country. Against white-collar professionals only, the same salary lands in the top 34%.

A operations manager comfortably clears the national median while sitting in the lower half of the professional cohort. If you benchmark against national averages you will conclude you are doing well; if you benchmark against colleagues in similar white-collar roles you will conclude the opposite. The second comparison is the one that matters when you negotiate.

What it actually affords

There is no personal income tax on salaries in the UAE, so gross pay is close to take-home. Rent is the cost that eats it. A typical one-bedroom in Dubai runs about AED 68,000 to AED 82,000 a year against an annual midpoint of AED 320,400 for this role. At roughly 26% of gross, a Dubai one-bedroom sits just inside the usual guidance of keeping rent under 30%, though not with much slack once schooling or a car is added.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a operations manager earn in Dubai?
Between AED 12,000 and AED 41,400 a month, with a midpoint around AED 26,700, based on published recruiter salary guides reduced by 10% to strip out package inflation. That is AED 320,400 a year at the midpoint.
Is AED 26,700 a month a good salary in the UAE?
It places you at the 92.6th percentile of all UAE workers, so in the top 7.4% of everyone employed in the country. Among white-collar professionals specifically it is the top 34%. Whether it feels good depends heavily on rent: a Dubai one-bedroom costs AED 68,000 to AED 82,000 a year.
How much experience do you need to earn the top of the operations manager range?
Roughly twelve years or more. Recruiter guides place early-career hires near AED 16,179 and people with 16 or more years near AED 39,930 for this role.
Do operations managers earn more in Dubai or Abu Dhabi?
Almost the same. Abu Dhabi runs about 2% below Dubai on our emirate index for professional roles, which is well within the variation between individual employers. The northern emirates run 8 to 12% lower.
