Average salary in Dubai, 2026

Updated 5 August 2026 · The UAE Wealth Dashboard · Methodology and sources

The average gross salary in Dubai is roughly AED 15,234 per month (AED 182,808 a year) for workers in regular households. But the average is misleading: the median worker in that same group earns AED 8,926 a month, and across the entire UAE workforce — including construction and domestic workers — the median falls to about AED 3,662.

Why does the average salary in Dubai have three different answers?

Almost every figure you will find online describes a different population, which is why published answers range from AED 3,600 to AED 20,000 a month. There are three honest answers:

PopulationMedianMean (average)
All workers (~9.2M, incl. labour camps and domestic staff)AED 3,662AED 9,490
Workers in regular households (~5.5M, official survey frame)AED 8,926AED 15,234
Professionals and executives (~2.0M, white-collar)AED 20,010AED 29,679

The UAE Labour Force Survey reports a median of about AED 10,000 and a mean near AED 16,000. Wage Protection System data, which covers registered private-sector workers, shows more than 60% of the workforce earning under AED 5,000 a month. Both are true. Household surveys do not reach labour camps and collective housing, where much of the low-wage workforce lives.

Is salary in Dubai tax free, and what does housing cost?

There is no personal income tax in the UAE, so gross pay is close to take-home. The main cost that eats it is rent: a typical one-bedroom in Dubai runs about AED 68,000–82,000 a year, roughly twice the equivalent in Sharjah or Ajman.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good salary in Dubai?
A salary of AED 20,000 a month puts you in roughly the top 11% of all UAE workers and around the median for white-collar professionals. AED 30,000 a month reaches about the top 6% of all workers. For a single person, AED 15,000–20,000 supports a comfortable lifestyle in most of Dubai; families typically need AED 30,000 or more once school fees and larger housing are included.
What is the average salary in Dubai per month?
About AED 15,234 per month as a mean for workers living in regular households, per the UAE Labour Force Survey frame. The median for that group is AED 8,926. Across the entire workforce including labour-camp residents, the median is closer to AED 3,662.
Is AED 10,000 a good salary in Dubai?
AED 10,000 a month places you above roughly 77% of all UAE workers, but near the bottom quartile of white-collar professionals. It is workable for a single person sharing accommodation and tight for a family in Dubai given rent levels.
Are salaries in Dubai tax free?
Yes. The UAE levies no personal income tax on salaries, so gross pay is effectively take-home pay. Corporate tax of 9% applies to business profits above AED 375,000, and 5% VAT applies to most goods and services, but neither is deducted from wages.
