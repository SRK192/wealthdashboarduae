What percentile is a quantity surveyor salary in the UAE?

AED 22,500 a month sits at the 90.5th percentile of all UAE workers, putting the role in the top 9.5% of everyone employed in the country. Measured against white-collar professionals only, the same salary lands in the top 43%.

A quantity surveyor comfortably clears the national median while sitting in the lower half of the professional cohort. If you benchmark against national averages you will conclude you are doing well; if you benchmark against colleagues in similar white-collar roles you will conclude the opposite. The second comparison is the one that matters when you negotiate.

Is a quantity surveyor salary taxed in the UAE?

No. There is no personal income tax on salaries anywhere in the UAE, so for an expatriate employee gross and net are the same figure: a quantity surveyor on the AED 22,500 midpoint takes home AED 22,500 a month, or AED 270,000 a year, with no statutory payroll deduction. Emirati nationals do have a pension deduction that expatriate staff do not, paid to the GPSSA or, in Abu Dhabi, to the Abu Dhabi Pension Fund. The 9% corporate tax introduced in June 2023 applies to business profits, not to employment income, and VAT of 5% is charged on spending rather than earnings.

Does the quantity surveyor salary range include housing allowance?

No. The AED 15,000 to AED 30,000 range on this page is base pay. Recruiter salary guides normally quote the full package, which bundles housing, transport and sometimes schooling on top of basic salary, so we reduce their published figures by 10% to get back to something comparable across employers. A UAE offer at this level is typically structured as a basic salary of roughly 50 to 60% of the total, with the remainder paid as allowances. That split matters more than it looks, because gratuity and several other entitlements are calculated on basic pay alone.

How much end-of-service gratuity does a quantity surveyor accrue?

Roughly AED 47,250 after five years and AED 114,750 after ten, on the AED 22,500 midpoint. Under Article 51 of Federal Decree-Law 33 of 2021, gratuity is 21 days of basic wage for each of the first five years of service and 30 days for each year after that, with a minimum of one year of continuous service and a total capped at two years' wage. The figures above assume basic pay is 60% of gross, which is a common UAE split but not a universal one, so check your own contract: gratuity is calculated on basic salary only, and allowances are excluded.

What can a quantity surveyor salary afford in Dubai?

A typical one-bedroom in Dubai runs about AED 68,000 to AED 82,000 a year, or 30% of the AED 270,000 annual midpoint for this role. At roughly 30% of gross, a Dubai one-bedroom breaches the usual 30% guidance. In practice people in this band share, move to Sharjah or Ajman and commute, or negotiate above the midpoint.
