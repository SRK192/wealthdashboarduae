How many millionaires are in Dubai and the UAE?

Updated 4 August 2026 · The UAE Wealth Dashboard · Methodology and sources

Dubai is home to about 81,200 resident dollar millionaires, 237 centi-millionaires and 20 billionaires. Across the UAE there are roughly 183,000 millionaires. The country has been the world's number one destination for millionaire migration four years running, with an estimated net inflow of 9,800 in 2025.

Dubai ranked 18th worldwide at end-2024 and was the biggest climber in the global top 50, with its millionaire population up 102% over the preceding decade.

Why are wealthy people moving to Dubai?

The drivers are consistent across reports: no personal income tax, golden visa residency of 5–10 years, political stability, direct global air connectivity, and a property market where prime Dubai residential values rose roughly 194% over five years. Dubai recorded 432 sales above $10 million in the twelve months to Q1 2025, the highest of any city globally.

Will Dubai's millionaire growth continue?

Knight Frank projects UAE ultra-high-net-worth individuals worth $30 million or more rising from 4,851 in 2026 to 6,588 by 2031. UBS projected UAE millionaire numbers growing about 15% between 2023 and 2028.

Frequently asked questions

How many millionaires live in Dubai?
About 81,200 resident US-dollar millionaires as of end-2024, according to Henley & Partners and New World Wealth, including 237 centi-millionaires worth $100 million or more and 20 billionaires.
Which country attracts the most millionaires?
The UAE, for four consecutive years. It recorded net inflows of 5,200 millionaires in 2022, 4,700 in 2023, 7,200 in 2024 and a projected 9,800 in 2025 — the highest of any country worldwide.
Why do millionaires move to Dubai?
No personal income tax on salaries or capital gains, long-term golden visa residency, political and currency stability (the dirham is pegged to the US dollar), strong global connectivity, and a fast-appreciating prime property market.
