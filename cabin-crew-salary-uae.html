What percentile is a cabin crew salary in the UAE?

AED 10,788 a month sits at the 78.7th percentile of all UAE workers, putting the role in the top 21% of everyone employed in the country. Measured against white-collar professionals only, the same salary lands in the bottom 17%.

A cabin crew earns above the national median but not by a wide margin, and sits well down the professional distribution. Roles in this band are where the cost of Dubai rent bites hardest, because pay clears the national average without clearing the housing market.

Is a cabin crew salary taxed in the UAE?

No. There is no personal income tax on salaries anywhere in the UAE, so for an expatriate employee gross and net are the same figure: a cabin crew on the AED 10,788 midpoint takes home AED 10,788 a month, or AED 129,450 a year, with no statutory payroll deduction. Emirati nationals do have a pension deduction that expatriate staff do not, paid to the GPSSA or, in Abu Dhabi, to the Abu Dhabi Pension Fund. The 9% corporate tax introduced in June 2023 applies to business profits, not to employment income, and VAT of 5% is charged on spending rather than earnings.

Does the cabin crew salary range include housing allowance?

No. The AED 9,538 to AED 12,038 range on this page is base pay. Recruiter salary guides normally quote the full package, which bundles housing, transport and sometimes schooling on top of basic salary, so we reduce their published figures by 10% to get back to something comparable across employers. A UAE offer at this level is typically structured as a basic salary of roughly 50 to 60% of the total, with the remainder paid as allowances. That split matters more than it looks, because gratuity and several other entitlements are calculated on basic pay alone.

How much end-of-service gratuity does a cabin crew accrue?

Roughly AED 22,654 after five years and AED 55,016 after ten, on the AED 10,788 midpoint. Under Article 51 of Federal Decree-Law 33 of 2021, gratuity is 21 days of basic wage for each of the first five years of service and 30 days for each year after that, with a minimum of one year of continuous service and a total capped at two years' wage. The figures above assume basic pay is 60% of gross, which is a common UAE split but not a universal one, so check your own contract: gratuity is calculated on basic salary only, and allowances are excluded.

What can a cabin crew salary afford in Dubai?

A typical one-bedroom in Dubai runs about AED 68,000 to AED 82,000 a year, or 63% of the AED 129,450 annual midpoint for this role. At roughly 63% of gross, a Dubai one-bedroom is out of reach on this salary alone. Shared accommodation, employer-provided housing or the northern emirates are the realistic options, which is exactly why so much of the workforce lives outside Dubai.
