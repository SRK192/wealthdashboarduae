What percentile is a data scientist salary in the UAE?

AED 38,025 a month sits at the 95.8th percentile of all UAE workers, putting the role in the top 4.2% of everyone employed in the country. Measured against white-collar professionals only, the same salary lands in the top 19%.

A data scientist comfortably clears the national median while sitting in the lower half of the professional cohort. If you benchmark against national averages you will conclude you are doing well; if you benchmark against colleagues in similar white-collar roles you will conclude the opposite. The second comparison is the one that matters when you negotiate.

Is a data scientist salary taxed in the UAE?

No. There is no personal income tax on salaries anywhere in the UAE, so for an expatriate employee gross and net are the same figure: a data scientist on the AED 38,025 midpoint takes home AED 38,025 a month, or AED 456,300 a year, with no statutory payroll deduction. Emirati nationals do have a pension deduction that expatriate staff do not, paid to the GPSSA or, in Abu Dhabi, to the Abu Dhabi Pension Fund. The 9% corporate tax introduced in June 2023 applies to business profits, not to employment income, and VAT of 5% is charged on spending rather than earnings.

Does the data scientist salary range include housing allowance?

No. The AED 33,300 to AED 42,750 range on this page is base pay. Recruiter salary guides normally quote the full package, which bundles housing, transport and sometimes schooling on top of basic salary, so we reduce their published figures by 10% to get back to something comparable across employers. A UAE offer at this level is typically structured as a basic salary of roughly 50 to 60% of the total, with the remainder paid as allowances. That split matters more than it looks, because gratuity and several other entitlements are calculated on basic pay alone.

How much end-of-service gratuity does a data scientist accrue?

Roughly AED 79,852 after five years and AED 193,928 after ten, on the AED 38,025 midpoint. Under Article 51 of Federal Decree-Law 33 of 2021, gratuity is 21 days of basic wage for each of the first five years of service and 30 days for each year after that, with a minimum of one year of continuous service and a total capped at two years' wage. The figures above assume basic pay is 60% of gross, which is a common UAE split but not a universal one, so check your own contract: gratuity is calculated on basic salary only, and allowances are excluded.

What can a data scientist salary afford in Dubai?

A typical one-bedroom in Dubai runs about AED 68,000 to AED 82,000 a year, or 18% of the AED 456,300 annual midpoint for this role. At roughly 18% of gross, a Dubai one-bedroom is a comfortable share of a data scientist's income, and this role has genuine room for saving or for a larger place.
