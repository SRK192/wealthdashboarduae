Updated 3 August 2026 · The UAE Wealth Dashboard · Methodology and sources

Frequently asked questions

What salary puts you in the top 1% in the UAE?
About AED 87,333 a month (AED 1,047,996 a year) in labour income, measured across all UAE workers. Including capital income, the World Inequality Database puts the top 1% threshold closer to AED 154,000 a month per adult.
What is the top 10% income in the UAE?
Roughly AED 21,674 a month, or about AED 260,088 a year, across the full workforce. Among white-collar professionals only, the top 10% starts near AED 55,520 a month.
Am I rich in Dubai on AED 30,000 a month?
AED 30,000 a month puts you in roughly the top 6% of all UAE workers and the top 28% of professionals. It affords a comfortable lifestyle but is well short of the wealth tier: the top 1% of UAE adults hold net worth above about AED 14.4 million.
